The Philosophy

Artisan Gastronomy Rooted in Nature

Founded with an unwavering commitment to culinary purity, Solstice & Sage celebrates the harmony between unrivaled terroir and progressive culinary technique. Our kitchen maintains direct partnerships with over twenty organic regional farms, fishermen who practice zero-bycatch hook-and-line harvesting, and boutique foragers across the Pacific coast.

Marcus Vance

Executive Chef & Co-Founder

“Every plate tells the story of our soil, the hands that cultivated it, and the fleeting beauty of each season.”
